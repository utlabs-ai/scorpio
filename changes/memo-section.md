zh: skill 讲清 memo 是什么、该放什么：人定的规矩、主题的规范、踩过的坑、主题当前的目标和做法放进去，随手在变的东西（刚写的代码、命令输出、任务进度）不放；写之前先看已有的，过时的替换或退掉，不越堆越多。
en: The skill now explains what the memo is for and what belongs in it: rules people set, the topic's conventions, known traps, and its current goal and approach, but not things that change as you work, such as fresh code, command output or task progress. Agents read the memo before adding and replace or retire stale entries instead of piling up more.
